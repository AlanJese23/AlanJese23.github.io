Science Outreach | Monterrey, Mexico

Astronomy as access.

Teaching astronomy to children became a way to connect scientific curiosity with free educational access and community impact.

Mission

Making science feel reachable.

A key priority in my career is expanding access to free education for students who otherwise might not have it. Through astronomy lessons, I helped children see science as something they could question, touch, and imagine themselves inside of.

Impact

  • Taught astronomy to children between 6 and 12 years old.
  • Reached and inspired more than 200 young students.
  • Used space science to build curiosity, confidence, and early scientific identity.
